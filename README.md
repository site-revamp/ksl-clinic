# KSL Clinic – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

KSL Clinic is a hair restoration provider serving patients across the UK and Ireland. Its services include FUE hair transplants, beard and eyebrow transplants, female hair restoration, PRP therapy and laser hair treatment.

The redesigned homepage presents KSL Clinic as a professional, patient-focused hair restoration provider. It prioritises treatment discovery, clinic locations and a clearer route towards booking a complimentary consultation.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Simplified Navigation and Treatment Discovery
The homepage organises treatment categories and clinic locations into a clearer hierarchy. This addresses the previous navigation complexity, helping visitors identify relevant treatments and find the appropriate clinic more easily.

### 2. Reduced Promotional Clutter
Repetitive urgency messages and finance promotions have been given less prominence. The revised presentation creates a cleaner, more balanced brand experience, allowing clinical services and patient support to take priority over competing promotional messages.

### 3. Focused Consultation Journey
The homepage establishes booking a free consultation as the primary conversion action, with telephone and WhatsApp contact presented as secondary options. This addresses the previous issue of competing calls to action and gives visitors a clearer next step.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Navigation and layout:** Treatment and location information follows a clearer structure.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Consultation booking receives stronger prominence, while secondary actions are less competing.
- **Content presentation:** Promotional messaging is streamlined to keep attention on treatments and patient care.
- **Brand consistency:** The overall presentation is cleaner and more professional, with less visual competition between messages.
- **User experience:** Visitors can move more directly from exploring treatments and locations to requesting a consultation.

## Project Objective

Create a more focused homepage that makes KSL Clinic’s hair restoration services easier to explore, presents the brand more professionally and guides prospective patients towards booking a complimentary consultation.
